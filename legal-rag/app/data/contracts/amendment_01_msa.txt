AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
Amends the Master Services Agreement between Acme Corp and Vendor Labs LLC.

ARTICLE 1 - AMENDMENTS
1.1 Replacement of Section 4.1. Section 4.1 of the Master Services Agreement is deleted and replaced with: Except as provided in Sections 4.2, 4.3 and 4.4, each party's aggregate liability shall not exceed two times (2x) the fees paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.
1.2 New Section 4.4 Data Breach. Vendor's liability for a breach of Article 7 is capped at three times (3x) the annual fees and is not subject to Section 4.1.
1.3 Effect. Except as amended here, the Master Services Agreement remains in full force. Where this Amendment conflicts with the Master Services Agreement, this Amendment controls.
