MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
Between Acme Corp ("Customer") and Vendor Labs LLC ("Vendor")
Governing law: State of Delaware

ARTICLE 1 - DEFINITIONS
1.1 Confidential Information means non-public business, technical, or financial information disclosed by either party under this Agreement.
1.2 Services means the software development and hosting services described in each Statement of Work.
1.3 Term means the period beginning on the Effective Date and ending on the date stated in Section 9.1.

ARTICLE 2 - PAYMENT
2.1 Fees. Customer shall pay the fees in each Statement of Work within thirty (30) days of invoice.
2.2 Late Payment. Overdue amounts accrue interest at 1.0% per month.

ARTICLE 4 - LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
4.1 Cap. Except as provided in Sections 4.2 and 4.3, each party's aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.
4.2 Exclusions. Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.
4.3 Carve-Outs. The cap in Section 4.1 does not apply to breach of Article 6 (Confidentiality), or to gross negligence or willful misconduct.

ARTICLE 6 - CONFIDENTIALITY
6.1 Obligations. Each party shall protect the other party's Confidential Information with at least reasonable care and use it only to perform this Agreement.
6.2 Duration. Obligations under this Article survive for five (5) years after termination.

ARTICLE 7 - DATA PROTECTION
7.1 Personal Data. Vendor shall process personal data only on documented instructions from Customer and shall notify Customer of a breach within 72 hours.

ARTICLE 9 - TERM AND TERMINATION
9.1 Initial Term. This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for three (3) years, unless terminated earlier under Section 9.2.
9.2 Termination for Cause. Either party may terminate on thirty (30) days written notice if the other party materially breaches and fails to cure within that period.
9.3 Auto-Renewal. This Agreement renews for successive one-year terms unless either party gives ninety (90) days notice of non-renewal.

ARTICLE 11 - GOVERNING LAW
11.1 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of laws principles.
