MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT
Between Acme Corp and Vendor Labs LLC

ARTICLE 1 - PURPOSE
1.1 Purpose. The parties wish to explore a possible software licensing relationship.

ARTICLE 2 - CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
2.1 Definition. Confidential Information includes source code, pricing, and product roadmaps disclosed in any form.
2.2 Exclusions. Information that is public through no fault of the receiving party is not Confidential Information.

ARTICLE 3 - TERM
3.1 Duration. This NDA lasts two (2) years from the Effective Date.
3.2 Survival. Obligations for trade secrets survive for as long as the information remains a trade secret.

ARTICLE 4 - REMEDIES
4.1 Injunctive Relief. A breach may cause irreparable harm, and the non-breaching party may seek an injunction without posting bond.
4.2 Limitation. Section 4.1 does not limit damages claims under the Master Services Agreement.
