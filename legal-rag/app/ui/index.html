            Metrics are lexical proxies: context recall and precision compare retrieved parent clauses with the ground-truth IDs; faithfulness is the share of answer words found in the retrieved context; answer recall is the share of expected-answer words found in the answer; relevancy is the share of question content words found in the answer. A case passes when all ground-truth clauses are retrieved, answer recall is at least 0.6, and the answer is not a refusal. Negative cases pass only when the system refuses.
